Privacy Policy for Cactara

Last updated: 26 September 2026
Developer: Minimal Habits

Cactara ("the app", "we", "us") is a habit tracker, budget planner and daily planner, with optional smoking-reduction tracking. Cactara was previously called Pace; only the name has changed, and this policy covers both. It explains what information is involved when you use Cactara, and what is — and is not — collected. Cactara is local-first: the information you enter stays on your device. We do not run a server that stores your personal data, and there is no account or sign-up.

1. Information you enter, stored only on your device

Everything you create in Cactara is saved in your device's local storage and is never transmitted to us or anyone else. This includes:

  • Your name, and an optional profile photo
  • Your habits, schedules, daily check-ins, streaks and goals — including water, steps, exercise, meditation, sleep and, if you use it, your smoking plan and logs
  • Your mood check-ins, gratitude entries, and check-in notes and photos
  • Your tasks, daily routines, reminders and Essentials checklist
  • Your budget and finances — income, expenses, bills, subscriptions, debts, savings goals and the currency you choose

Photos you attach (a profile picture or a photo on a check-in note) are picked from your device and stored inside the app; they are not uploaded anywhere. This data remains on your device until you delete it in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall Cactara — at which point it is gone. Because it never leaves your device, we cannot access, recover, or delete it for you.

If you choose to export your money history (CSV), Cactara creates the file on your device and opens your phone's share sheet. Where that file goes is your choice; we never receive a copy.

Cactara uses the services below to do specific jobs. We do not receive your personal data from them.

3. Health Connect (optional)

Cactara can fill in some habits for you from Health Connect. This is entirely opt-in: nothing is read until you turn it on for a habit and grant permission. Cactara requests read-only access to:

  • Steps — to complete your Walking habit
  • Exercise (exercise sessions) — to complete your Exercise habit
  • Hydration — to fill your Water habit
  • Cactara reads only these data types, only for today's progress, and never writes any data to Health Connect.
  • This data is used solely on your device to tick off your habits. It is never transmitted off your device, shared with third parties, sold, or used for advertising.
  • You can revoke access at any time in your device's Health Connect settings, or by turning auto-fill off in Cactara.

Cactara's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

4. App lock (biometric)

You can optionally protect the Finance area of Cactara with your device's fingerprint or face unlock. This check is performed entirely by your device's operating system — Cactara only receives a "success" or "failure" result and never sees or stores your fingerprint, face, or any other biometric data.

5. Notifications and widgets

If you enable reminders, Cactara schedules notifications locally on your device. Home-screen widgets read the same on-device data. Neither is sent through any server we operate.

6. Wear OS watch (optional)

If you use a Cactara companion app on a paired Wear OS watch, today's habit data is sent directly from your phone to your watch through Google's Wearable Data Layer. It does not pass through any server we operate.

7. Analytics

Cactara does not include any analytics or user-tracking service of our own. Google Play may collect standard diagnostic and crash information at the platform level; this is governed by Google's policies and your device settings.

8. Children

Cactara is intended for adults managing their own habits and money, and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Your choices, rights, and data deletion

Cactara has no account and no server, so there is no profile for us to delete — you control your data directly:

  • Your app data (including your budget, finances, photos, and Health Connect readings): delete individual items in the app, clear the app's storage in your device settings, or uninstall Cactara to erase all of it at once. Because it never leaves your device, this permanently removes it.
  • Ads: subscribing to Premium removes ads entirely. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you can change your ad consent choice at any time. You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in your device settings.
  • Purchases: manage or cancel a subscription in your Google Play account. To request deletion of data held by a third-party service, contact that service using the links in Section 2.
  • Suggestions you sent: email us (Section 12) and we will delete them.
  • Questions or deletion requests: email us at the address in Section 12 and we will help.

10. Data retention

We retain none of your personal data, because we never receive it. The only exception is a suggestion you choose to send us, which we keep until you ask us to delete it. Your app data persists on your device until you remove it. Data held by the third-party services above is retained under their policies.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the current version. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
