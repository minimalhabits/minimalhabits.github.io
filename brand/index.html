How we make things

Four rules we make things by

Every app and game passes all four before it ships. They are also the test we apply to work we make for other people.

Rule 1

One job

Every app is built around one job. Anything that does not serve it waits for a later version, or never ships.

Rule 2

No manual

If a screen needs a tooltip to explain it, the screen gets another pass, not another tooltip.

Rule 3

Less on screen

We remove before we add. Fewer buttons and fewer choices leave more room for the thing you came to do.

Rule 4

Done properly

Spacing, wording, the sound a tap makes, what happens offline. We ship when it is finished.

The dot

One orange dot, always solid

The dot is the brand's moving subject. It is the full stop of our headlines, and in our films it is the thing you follow.

Know what's left
before it goes

How the dot behaves

  • It ends a headline as its full stop, sized to the type.
  • In films it has a job in every scene: it presses a button, ticks a day, draws a ring, pops what is in the way.
  • It can grow into a circle that holds one line, then shrink back into a full stop.
  • Every film ends with the dot coming home to the m.
  • Only one orange dot is on screen at a time. It is never glass, never outlined and never another colour.

Motion

Everything moves on springs

Things press, settle and arrive the way objects do. Something is always moving, but words hold still while they are read.

Damping 1 · settles without overshoot
Damping 0.75 · arrives with momentum
  • Springs, not timers. Response 0.4–0.6 s. Damping 1 for anything that settles; 0.75–0.85 only for things that arrive with momentum, like a card dropping in.
  • Always some life. Glass dots drift, a ring turns slowly, the dot travels.
  • Readable text. A line holds still long enough to read it twice.
  • Follow the dot. Scenes join by following the dot, not by cutting.
  • Centred. The action sits on the middle of the frame.

Sound

Intriguing, never playful

Every film gets its own score and its own effects, and nothing is reused from one film to the next.

What we reach for

  • Low drones and slow pads in a large room
  • Felt piano, bowed glass, choir, pizzicato strings
  • Glass pings and crystal notes for the dots
  • A riser, then one deep impact at the turn of the film
  • A soft bell when the dot comes home

What we leave out

  • Cartoon pops, boings and bouncy plucks
  • Upbeat house and stock "happy" music
  • Vinyl crackle, square-wave buzzes and harsh noise
  • Any click or distortion: every mix is checked before it ships

Voice

We, in plain words

We speak as the studio, with normal capitals and short sentences. We say who made it and what it costs.

  • Speak as "we", the studio.

  • Never invent users, reviews or numbers.

The frame

Reels and Shorts are 1080 × 1920 at 30 frames a second. The apps cover the edges, so the story lives in the middle.

  • First second: the viewer's problem, as text on screen.
  • Keep clear of the top 250 px, the bottom 420 px, the right-hand strip below y 1000, and the left 48 px.
  • End on the app's icon for an app film, or on the m for a studio film, with the dot coming home.
  • Captions name the app ("Fondo by Minimal Habits") and say what is free and what is paid.
