Questions

Good to know

What does it cost?

It depends on what your app needs to do. Tell us the one job, and we will reply with a plan and a quote before any work starts.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the job. You get a timeline with the quote, and keeping the app to one job keeps that timeline honest.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone, iPad and Android phones and tablets, from one project.

Can it be in my language?

Yes. Our own apps ship in English, Spanish and Arabic, including right-to-left layouts, and we can add the languages your customers speak.

How does the social media service work?

We set it up inside your own Claude account and connect it to the scheduling tool you use. Each week it prepares your posts and captions, then waits. Nothing goes out until you approve it, and your accounts and content stay yours.

What happens after launch?

We can keep improving it with you: fixes, new versions, and updates for new phones and new store rules.
