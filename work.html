    Questions

    Good to know

    What does it cost?

    Each service above shows where its price starts, and adding services to your list keeps a running total. An app or a game depends on what it needs to do, so tell us the one job and we reply with a plan and a quote before any work starts.

    How do payments work?

    Half when we start, and half when the work is delivered. Monthly services, such as social media and monthly care, are billed each month.

    Are there costs on top of your price?

    Only the things that stay in your name. For a website, the hosting and the domain. For the AI studio, your own AI subscription. For the AI studio and social media, tools in your name, such as your social media scheduler. For an app, Apple and Google charge their own fees for a developer account.

    Can I book one service, or combine a few?

    Both. Pick the services you want, combine as many as you like and add any extras. We send you one quote with the full price.

    How long does it take?

    It depends on the size of the job. You get a timeline with the quote, and keeping the app to one job keeps that timeline honest.

    How many rounds of changes are included?

    Two rounds of changes are included in every service. More can be added, and we quote them first.

    Who owns the app, the code and the designs?

    You do. Once the final payment is made, the app, the code, the designs and the store listing are yours, published under your own developer account.

    Which devices does it run on?

    iPhone, iPad and Android phones and tablets, from one project.

    Can it be in my language?

    Yes. Our own apps ship in English, Spanish and Arabic, including right-to-left layouts, and we can add the languages your customers speak.

    Do people need to download a mini-game?

    No. A branded mini-game plays from a link in any phone browser, so people can share it and play straight away, with no app store needed.

    Do you work with clients outside Jordan?

    Yes. We work remotely with clients anywhere, in English or Arabic.

    What happens after launch?

    We can keep improving it with you: fixes, new versions, and updates for new phones and new store rules. For the AI studio, monthly care adds new skills and video styles, fixes and updates.

    Which AI do you use?

    A range of AI tools, chosen for the job you want done. Once we start working together, we recommend the right mix for what you need, your team and your workload.

    Do I get to check things before they go out?

    That is up to you. It depends on the kind of content or service, and on how hands-on you want to be: you can check every post before it goes out, or let a planned week run on its own.

    How does social media automation work?

    You get our set of tools for planning, writing, making and scheduling your posts, set up around your brand. Each week they prepare your content and line it up in your scheduler, and you choose how much you check before it goes out. Your accounts and your content stay yours.
