Privacy Policy for Jellup

Last updated: 17 November 2026
Developer: Minimal Habits

Jellup: Don't Pop the Balloon ("the game", "we", "us") is a one-thumb game: puff up and shrink a soft balloon to fall through a cave without popping. This policy explains what information is involved when you play, and what is, and is not, collected. Jellup has no account and no sign-up, and we do not run a server that stores your personal data.

1. Your progress, stored only on your device

Everything the game keeps is saved on your device and is never sent to us. This includes your best score and deepest depth, your level and experience, missions, medals and streak, the balloons and trails you have unlocked or bought, which ones you use, the checkpoints you have reached, and your settings (sounds, music, vibration, way to play).

This data stays on your device until you use Reset progress in Settings, clear the app's storage, or uninstall Jellup. Because it never leaves your device, we cannot see, recover or delete it for you. Items you bought are tied to your App Store or Google Play account and can be restored with Restore purchases.

2. Information handled by third-party services

Jellup uses the services below for specific jobs. We do not receive your personal data from them.

ServicePurposeWhat it may collect
Google AdMob Shows ads (a banner on the menus, an occasional full-screen ad between runs, and an ad you choose to watch for one more go or a run from a checkpoint) to players who have not bought Remove ads Advertising identifier, device information and approximate (coarse) location, used to serve and measure ads, and, with your permission, to show personalized ads (chosen using your activity in other apps and websites). On iPhone, your advertising identifier is used only if you choose Allow when Jellup asks to track; if you don’t, you still see ads, just not personalized ones. Players in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before any ad is requested, and ads are personalized only if they agree.
RevenueCat Manages in-app purchases Purchase history and an app-generated anonymous identifier. It does not receive your name or your game progress.
Apple App Store / Google Play Billing Process payments for purchases Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google. Jellup never sees or stores your card or payment details.
Apple Game Center (iPhone) Leaderboards (best score, deepest depth) and achievements If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple with your Game Center player name, and are shown to other players on the leaderboards. You can sign out of Game Center, or change who sees you, in your iPhone's settings.
Google Play Games (Android) Leaderboards (best score, deepest depth) and achievements If you are signed in to Play Games, your scores and achievements are sent to Google with your Play Games profile, and are shown to other players on the leaderboards. You can sign out of Play Games, or change who sees your profile, in the Google Play Games settings on your device.

These services handle the data they collect under their own privacy policies:

  • Google (AdMob, Play and Play Games): policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat: revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple (App Store and Game Center): apple.com/legal/privacy

3. Messages and links

Request a balloon opens your own email app with a message to us; you decide whether to send it, and we receive only what you send. Follow us opens our pages on Instagram, TikTok and YouTube in their own apps or your browser, under their own privacy policies.

4. Analytics

Apart from the ads described in Section 2, Jellup includes no analytics or tracking service. Apple and Google may collect standard diagnostic and crash information at the platform level, under their policies and your device settings.

5. Children

Jellup is made for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has played and that our ad partner collected their information, email us and we will help have it removed.

6. Your choices and data deletion

  • Your progress: Reset progress in Settings, clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling Jellup erases it for good.
  • Ads: buying Remove ads removes them. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you can change your ad consent at any time with Ad privacy choices in Settings. On iPhone you can turn tracking off for Jellup at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device’s ad settings. With tracking off, ads are no longer personalized.
  • Game Center: sign out, or change your profile's visibility, in your iPhone's settings.
  • Play Games: sign out, or change your profile's visibility, in the Google Play Games settings on your Android device.
  • Questions or deletion requests for data held by a third-party service: contact that service using the links in Section 2, or email us and we will help.

7. Data retention

We retain none of your personal data, because we never receive it. The only exception is an email you choose to send us, which we keep until you ask us to delete it.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game changes. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the current version.
